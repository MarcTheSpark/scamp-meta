Music Created using SCAMP
=========================

Here are a few examples of pieces of music recently created in SCAMP. More coming soon!


Gabriel Dufour-Laperrière: `Πάντα ῥεῖ (Pánta rhei), for string quartet <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SqBdKFO0KYc>`_
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. raw:: html

    <iframe class="youtube" width="560" height="315" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/SqBdKFO0KYc" title="YouTube video player" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen></iframe>

Performed by Quatuor Bozzini. According to Gabriel, "Each section of the piece was composed through SCAMP, coding and working out each algorithm until I was satisfied with the result. I then assembled the good bits into a coherent whole. SCAMP let me further develop my musical processes and gain better control over them. It was also flexible enough to implement a specific microtonal notation that the musicians wanted."
